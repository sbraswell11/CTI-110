
# Turkey Sandwich

## Overview
- Bread
- Mayo
- Turkey
- Cheese
- Lettuce
- Tomatos
- Cucumbers

## Assumptions
- The robot will be able to pick up things and open jars ect.
- The robot may place ingredients unproportioned 
- the robot may leave all jars unopened and out
- the slice of tomato will be big maybe with plastic on it

## Numbered Steps
1. get two pieces of bread 
2. spread mayo on one side of both slices of bread
3. put three slices of turkey on one slice of bread 
4. put cheese on the turkey
5. put one slice of lettuce on the cheese
6. put one slice of tomato on the lettuce
7. put four slices of cucumbers on the tomato
8. put the second piece of bread on top


## Done when
- final piece of bread is on top
